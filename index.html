About us

A cove for slow mornings and long afternoons.

KAVE was built around one idea: coffee, bakes and a bit of studio space, all under one roof — arched, sunlit, and quietly coastal in feel, even here along Arnaldo Highway.

Every cup is pulled to order, our matcha is whisked from ceremonial-grade leaves, and the pastry case is restocked through the day. Pull up a chair at the communal tables, or tuck into a corner booth — either way, there's no rush.
